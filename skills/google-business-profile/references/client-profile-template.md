# Client profile template

Save as `client-profile.md` (or `clients/<name>/client-profile.md` for agencies with several
clients). Other marketing skills can read the same file, so keep it factual and current.
Leave a field blank rather than guessing.

```markdown
# Client profile: [Business name]

_Last updated: [date]_

## Basics
- **What they do**: [one sentence]
- **Website**: [URL] · **Sitemap**: [URL if known]
- **Service area**: [city / region / list of areas they actually serve]
- **Locations**: [addresses if storefront]
- **Phone / booking**: [how customers book]
- **Google Business Profile**: [URL if known]

## Services
| Service | Typical price range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [service] | [range] | [what changes the price, what's included] |

## Customers
- **Main customer types**: [e.g. homeowners in older houses, busy pet owners, property managers]
- **Top questions customers ask**: [list, in their words]
- **Top objections / worries**: [price, trust, mess, time...]
- **Why customers choose them**: [from reviews or the owner]

## Proof
- **Years in business**: [n]
- **Licences / certifications / insurance**: [list]
- **Review highlights**: [2–3 short quotes, with permission]
- **Photo library**: [where real job photos live]
- **Case stories**: [short anonymised stories]

## Voice
- **Tone**: [e.g. friendly and plain-spoken, no jargon]
- **Words to use**: [their terms]
- **Words to avoid**: [competitor names, jargon, claims they can't make]
- **Real offers / guarantees**: [only ones that actually exist]

## Local knowledge
- **Conditions that matter**: [climate, housing stock, seasonality]
- **Rebates / rules**: [anything local]
- **Busy season**: [when they're booked out]

## Content so far
- **Existing posts**: [titles / URLs]
- **What's worked**: [posts or topics that brought calls]
- **Topics to avoid**: [anything]
```
