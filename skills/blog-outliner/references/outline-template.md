# Outline template

Use this structure for the saved outline. Headings in `[brackets]` are filled in; drop any
optional part that doesn't apply rather than filling it with filler.

```markdown
# Outline: [Working title]

## Brief
- **Business**: [name, what they do, service area if local]
- **Reader**: [who, awareness level]
- **Search intent**: [learn / compare / price / find provider / DIY]
- **Main keyword**: [phrase] · **Variants**: [2–4 related phrases people actually use]
- **Goal**: [what the business gets from this post]
- **CTA**: [the one action, and the mechanism: phone / booking link / quote form]
- **Estimated length**: [range], driven by [why]

## Angle
**Chosen**: [one-line angle] — [why it fits this reader and this business]
**Considered**: [angle 2] · [angle 3] (one line each on why not)

## Title options
1. [Primary: clear, specific, matches the search, promises the angle]
2. [Alternative]
3. [Alternative]
**Meta description**: [~150 characters, answers the query and gives a reason to click]

## What we know that they don't (information gain)
- [Item we already have] → used in [section]
- [Item that depends on the owner] → [Q#] → needed for [section]

## Questions for the owner (max 8, paste-ready)
1. [Most valuable question, phrased as you'd text it]
2. ...
**Nice to have**: [one line, unnumbered]

## What's already out there (research summary, ≤ 8 bullets)
- **Expected ground** (most top results cover it): [topics]
- **Where they fall short**: [vague, outdated, generic, no local numbers, no proof...]
- **Questions people ask** (PAA, forums, reviews): [the 3–5 that matter, in the reader's words]
- **AI answers / snippets**: [format being rewarded, sources cited]

---

## Outline

### Intro
- **Answer first**: [the 2–3 sentence direct answer to the main question, with a real number
  where the question is about one; use a sourced market figure if the owner's isn't known yet]
- **Hook / why trust us**: [the first-hand detail or proof that earns the next paragraph]

### H2: [Reader's question or plain statement]
- **Purpose**: [why this section exists, one line]
- **Opening answer**: [the direct answer that starts the section]
- **Cover**:
  - [point]
  - [point]
- **Proof / information gain**: [specific item, or Q#]
- **H3s** (only if the section genuinely splits): [list]
- **Visual** (optional): [photo / table / chart, and what it shows]

[repeat H2 blocks as the topic needs]

### CTA placement
- **Mid-post**: after [section], because [reader is ready here]: "[CTA line]"
- **End**: "[CTA line]"

### FAQ (optional; only for real questions not answered above)
- [Question] → [answer direction]

---

## Internal links
- **Link to**: [page URL or title] — anchor "[text]" — from section [X]
- **Should link here**: [existing page] — anchor "[text]"

## Repurposing plan
- **Social 1** ([platform]): hook "[line]" · point: [from section X] · CTA: [..]
- **Social 2** ...
- **Google Business Profile post** (local): [one-paragraph brief]
- **Short video idea**: [what to film, from which section]
- **Email line**: [one sentence teaser]

## Assumptions to confirm
- [Anything you decided without the user's input]

## Notes for the writer (≤ 6 bullets)
- [Tone, words to use or avoid, claims that need checking, anything from the profile]
```

## Notes

- The "Considered" angles stay in the file on purpose. They are cheap future posts.
- Keep research notes to patterns. The writer needs "every top result skips cost" more than a
  per-URL summary.
- If local mode is off, drop the Google Business Profile line and any local-only fields.
